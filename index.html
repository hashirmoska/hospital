Our story

Founded in 1952, Floravia Hospital began as a small community clinic with only 12 beds and a handful of dedicated physicians. Its mission was simple yet profound: to bring compassionate, modern healthcare to families who previously had limited access to medical services. By the 1970s, the hospital expanded into a full-service medical center, adding specialized departments in pediatrics, cardiology, and surgery. This growth was fueled by generous donations from local residents and the tireless work of volunteers who believed in building a healthier future. In the 1990s, Starlight General became one of the first hospitals in the region to adopt advanced diagnostic technologies, including MRI and CT scanning. This commitment to innovation positioned the hospital as a leader in patient care and medical research. Today, with over 500 beds, 1,200 staff members, and state-of-the-art facilities, Starlight General Hospital continues to honor its founding vision. It serves as both a healing center and a teaching institution, training the next generation of doctors and nurses while providing world-class care to thousands of patients each year.

Essential Patient Care Policies

Emergency Guidelines

Our hospital follows strict emergency guidelines to ensure every patient receives fast, safe, and appropriate care during urgent situations. When a patient arrives in the emergency department, they are assessed based on the seriousness of their condition rather than the order of arrival.

Patient Rights

Every patient in our hospital has the right to receive respectful, compassionate, and professional care at all times.

Privacy and Confidentiality

Protecting patient information is one of our highest priorities. All personal and medical details are stored securely and accessed only by authorized healthcare professionals involved in the patient’s care. We follow strict confidentiality rules to ensure that sensitive information is never shared without the patient’s permission, except when required for safety or legal reasons. Our staff is trained to handle records responsibly, maintain secure communication, and safeguard digital data. These privacy standards help build trust between patients and medical teams, ensuring that everyone feels safe and respected when receiving care.

Infection Control

To maintain a safe and healthy environment, our hospital follows strong infection control policies designed to protect patients, visitors, and staff. These measures include regular hand‑washing, proper use of sanitizers, wearing protective equipment when necessary, and keeping all medical areas clean and disinfected. Visitors may be asked to follow additional safety rules during high‑risk periods, such as wearing masks or limiting contact with vulnerable patients. Our staff works continuously to monitor hygiene practices, reduce contamination risks, and prevent the spread of infections within the hospital. These efforts help ensure that everyone receives care in a clean, safe, and well‑maintained environment.
